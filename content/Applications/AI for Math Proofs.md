## Notable tools / instances
- AlphaProof Nexus (Google DeepMind) — formal proof search system; solved 9/353 formalized Erdős problems and 44/492 OEIS conjectures (2026), explicitly framed by DeepMind as bounded, not general mathematical capability
- **AlphaGeometry: Solving olympiad geometry without human demonstrations** (Trinh, Wu, Le, He, Luong — DeepMind, Nature 2024) — neuro-symbolic geometry solver, the paper that established the "verified formal search" approach this field's whole "Mathematical Superintelligence" framing builds on
- **AlphaProof/AlphaGeometry 2 at IMO 2024** — the pairing achieved silver-medal-equivalent performance, solving 4 of 6 2024 International Mathematical Olympiad problems (including the hardest, solved by only five human contestants); still the highest-profile concrete result the field points to as evidence formal-proof AI has left the toy-problem stage

## Key players
- [[Harmonic]] — Aristotle, Lean 4-verified "Mathematical Superintelligence," IMO gold-medal level in 2025
- [[Axiom]] — AxiomProver, applies the same Lean-verification approach to enterprise code/finance/chip design rather than competition math
- [[Project Numina]] — open nonprofit alternative to the commercial labs; Numina-Lean-Agent solved all 12 Putnam 2025 problems
- [[Xena Project (Imperial, Kevin Buzzard)]] — academic lab formalizing competition and research-level mathematics in Lean; Buzzard's Erdős-problems formalization effort is the direct substrate AlphaProof Nexus's Erdős results above are measured against
