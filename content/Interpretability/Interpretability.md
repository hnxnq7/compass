## Interpretability

Understanding what's actually happening inside a model, causally, rather than just observing its outputs — increasingly framed as an auditing/safety discipline, not just pure research curiosity.

## Cross-cutting notes
- 2026 framing: "understanding" now means building causal hypotheses, testing them with interventions, and validating against explicit benchmarks — not just finding a correlation and calling it a feature.
- The field is expanding past text-only LLMs into audio, code, and multimodal models, and into practical debugging/safety tooling rather than staying purely academic.
