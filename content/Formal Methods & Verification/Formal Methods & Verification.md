## Formal Methods & Verification

Proving things about software, hardware, and math instead of just testing them — and the fast-moving question of how much of that proving work AI agents can now do themselves.

## Cross-cutting notes
- The 2026 shift: formal verification used to be a bottleneck because it required rare specialist labor. Agentic tooling (LLM-generated SVAs, autoformalization for Lean) is attacking that labor bottleneck directly, in both hardware and math.
- "Spec-driven coding" is the software-engineering-adjacent cousin of formal verification: same underlying idea (a precise spec as source of truth) but aimed at velocity/correctness for ordinary code rather than mathematical proof. Worth tracking as one interconnected cluster, not two separate worlds.
