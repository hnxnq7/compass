## Xena Project (Imperial College London, Kevin Buzzard)

*Kevin Buzzard's group, best known for leading the formalization of Fermat's Last Theorem in Lean.*

## What they do
- Formalizes serious modern mathematics in Lean, aiming to get graduate-level and research-level math (not just undergraduate curricula) into a machine-checkable form
- Leading the multi-year project to formalize Andrew Wiles's proof of Fermat's Last Theorem in Lean — one of the most ambitious formalization efforts undertaken, with many major components already complete
- Broader mission is partly cultural: convincing working mathematicians that formalization is worth the effort, not just a computer-science curiosity

## Where they fit
- One of the highest-profile academic efforts inside [[Proof Assistants & Formal Mathematics]], and a useful contrast to AI-driven provers like AlphaProof — this is primarily human-driven formalization at the frontier of pure math, which is exactly the kind of proof corpus that trains and validates AI provers

## Notable work / recent moves
- Fermat's Last Theorem formalization project ongoing, with major components complete as of 2026
