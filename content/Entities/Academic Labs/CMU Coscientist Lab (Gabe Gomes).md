## CMU Coscientist Lab (Gabe Gomes)

*Gabe Gomes' chemistry/chemical-engineering lab at Carnegie Mellon — built Coscientist, one of the first LLM-driven systems to autonomously plan and physically execute a real chemistry experiment.*

## What they do
- Builds LLM-driven systems that plan chemical experiments, then hand the plan directly to robotic lab hardware to execute it — closing the loop from hypothesis to physical result, not just to a written proposal
- Works with CMU's cloud lab (opened 2024, the first university-affiliated cloud lab), giving the group and collaborators remote access to 200+ pieces of automated lab equipment

## Where they fit
- The clearest academic instance of this field's "what solutions feel inevitable" claim — closed-loop AI-proposes/lab-executes systems — running in a real chemistry lab rather than a simulation
- Predates and helped motivate the current wave of "AI Scientist" / closed-loop discovery systems ([[Sakana AI]], [[Periodic Labs]]) with a peer-reviewed, physically-validated result

## Notable work / recent moves
- **Autonomous chemical research with large language models** (Boiko, MacKnight, Kennedy, Gomes; Nature 2023) — Coscientist used GPT-4 and other LLMs to autonomously plan and execute palladium-catalyzed cross-coupling reaction optimization via robotic APIs
- Continued integration with CMU's cloud lab for further autonomous experimentation
