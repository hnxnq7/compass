## Schmidt Lab (Stanford)

*Ludwig Schmidt's research group (Stanford, with a joint appointment at Anthropic) — lead academic voice behind DataComp, the reference benchmark for data-curation research.*

## What they do
- Led DataComp (NeurIPS 2023 Datasets & Benchmarks) and DataComp-LM (2024): benchmarks that fix the training procedure and let researchers compete purely on dataset filtering/curation, isolating curation as the variable that matters rather than another axis buried inside a model paper
- DataComp-1B, the best baseline dataset from the effort, trained a CLIP ViT-L/14 to 79.2% zero-shot ImageNet accuracy, beating OpenAI's original CLIP by 3.7 points on identical compute — direct evidence that curation alone, not scale or architecture, was the lever
- Schmidt moved from University of Washington to a joint Stanford/Anthropic appointment, bridging academic curation research and frontier-lab practice

## Where they fit
- The academic anchor for [[Data Curation & Filtering]] that treats curation as a first-class, competitively benchmarked research problem — the empirical counterpart to AI2's fully-open toolchain (Dolma/OLMo) and Common Crawl's raw corpus

## Notable work / recent moves
- DataComp-LM extended the approach to language-model pretraining data, not just CLIP/image-text pairs
- Continues active publication on dataset scaling laws and filtering methodology from the joint Stanford/Anthropic position
