## UToPiA (UT Austin, Işıl Dillig)

*Işıl Dillig's group at UT Austin — program synthesis and program analysis/verification, with a recent emphasis on neurosymbolic synthesis.*

## What they do
- Develops techniques and tools to change how software is developed: program analysis/verification to surface subtle bugs and security vulnerabilities, and program synthesis to automatically generate programs from formal or informal specs
- Recent research direction is explicitly neurosymbolic — bridging LLM-style perception/pattern-matching with classical symbolic reasoning for synthesis, rather than treating the two as competitors
- Active at top PL venues (PLDI, POPL, FSE) with ongoing publication record through 2026

## Where they fit
- A second strong academic anchor for [[Program Synthesis]] alongside [[MIT CAP Group (Solar-Lezama Lab)|MIT CAP]] — Dillig's neurosymbolic framing is one of the more concrete instances of the "hybrid LLM + classical synthesis" trend the field note flags as inevitable
- Also touches [[Software Verification]] via the group's program-analysis/verification work, though synthesis is the closer fit
