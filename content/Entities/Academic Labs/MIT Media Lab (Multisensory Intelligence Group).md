## MIT Media Lab (Multisensory Intelligence Group)

*Paul Pu Liang's group at the MIT Media Lab — studies the foundations of multisensory AI, from sensing hardware through fusion models to human-AI interaction.*

## What they do
- Research group founded/directed by Paul Pu Liang, organized around three themes: sensing (extending how AI captures the world beyond digital/text input), a principled science for quantifying multimodal heterogeneity and interaction, and learning synergy between modalities (and between humans and AI)
- Builds both sensing hardware (e.g. tactile sensing gloves) and the computational models that fuse touch, vision, sound, and other channels
- Applies the work to productivity, creative expression, and health/wellbeing — e.g. AI systems that read affect and engagement in classroom, therapy, and eldercare settings without relying on words alone

## Where they fit
- The most direct academic anchor for [[Multisensory Integration]] — one of the few groups treating "fuse more than two senses into one coherent model" as the central research question, rather than a side effect of vision-language scaling
- Published "A Vision for Multisensory Intelligence: Sensing, Science, and Synergy" (2026), which functions as a field manifesto/roadmap for the topic
