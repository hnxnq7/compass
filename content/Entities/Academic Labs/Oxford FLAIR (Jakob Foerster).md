## Oxford FLAIR (Foerster Lab for AI Research)

*Jakob Foerster's Oxford group — one of the foundational multi-agent reinforcement learning labs, now folding into a new UK AI research lab Foerster is launching at Oxford.*

## What they do
- Multi-agent reinforcement learning research: coordination, communication protocols, and emergent behavior between learning agents, plus meta-learning and open-ended training
- Built widely-used open infrastructure (JaxMARL) for running MARL experiments at GPU-native speed rather than the CPU-bound simulators that dominated the field before

## Where they fit
- This is the academic counterpart to the note's central question — showing genuine coordination gains over a single strong agent, and designing protocols that don't just recreate single-agent reliability problems one level up — for RL-trained (not just LLM-prompted) multi-agent systems
- JaxMARL's speedups made rigorous multi-agent coordination experiments tractable at a scale closer to what production multi-agent products now claim informally

## Notable work / recent moves
- **JaxMARL: Multi-Agent RL Environments and Algorithms in JAX** — end-to-end GPU-accelerated MARL library, reported up to 12,500x speedups over CPU-based environments, now widely used as a research substrate
- FLAIR is winding down as a standalone lab as of 2026, with Foerster launching a new Oxford AI research lab — worth watching for how the group's multi-agent research agenda carries over
