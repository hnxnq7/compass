## NYU Xie Lab (Saining Xie)

*Saining Xie's group at NYU — vision-centric multimodal learning, with visual-spatial reasoning as a core throughline; ran the NYU/Yale/Stanford collaboration (with Fei-Fei Li) that produced VSI-Bench.*

## What they do
- Builds vision-centric multimodal LLM systems (Cambrian-1, Cambrian-S) that treat visual representation quality as a first-class problem, not an afterthought to the language backbone
- Created VSI-Bench and its "Thinking in Space" analysis — a video-based benchmark testing whether multimodal LLMs can perceive, remember, and recall 3D spaces from egocentric video, distinct from image-centric VQA
- Ongoing work (Cambrian-S, ICLR 2026; Solaris) pushes toward "spatial supersensing" and multiplayer video world models

## Where they fit
- The clearest academic anchor for [[Spatial Intelligence]] specifically — VSI-Bench is the reference benchmark for measuring whether MLLMs have genuine 3D spatial cognition (vs. surface pattern-matching), the exact "solutions feel fake" test this field's note already flags; the project was a direct NYU/Yale/Stanford collaboration with Fei-Fei Li, the field's most visible industry figure via World Labs

## Notable work / recent moves
- "Thinking in Space: How Multimodal Large Language Models See, Remember, and Recall Spaces" (Yang, Yang, Gupta, Han, Fei-Fei Li, Saining Xie; NYU/Yale/Stanford, CVPR 2025) — introduced VSI-Bench
- Found MLLMs meaningfully subhuman on spatial cognition even as they saturate other visual benchmarks — the "renderer vs. genuine spatial reasoning" gap this field's note is centrally about
- Cambrian-S (ICLR 2026) extends the line toward "spatial supersensing" in continuous video
