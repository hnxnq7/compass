## Berkeley Robot Learning Lab (Pieter Abbeel)

*Pieter Abbeel's lab at UC Berkeley — one of the academic origins of model-based RL world models (PlaNet, Dreamer-lineage work), predating the current wave of video-generation "world model" products.*

## What they do
- Develops model-based reinforcement learning methods where an agent learns a compact predictive model of environment dynamics and plans/imagines inside it, rather than learning a policy from raw environment interaction alone
- Advised and collaborated on the Dreamer lineage (PlaNet, Dreamer, DreamerV3) and continues publishing on world-model-for-robotics methods (e.g. 2026 work surveying and extending world models for robot learning)
- Bridges model-based RL and large-scale generative video modeling — the same "world model" label current video-generation companies use, but from the control-and-planning side rather than the visual-fidelity side

## Where they fit
- The main academic counterpoint, inside [[Video Generation & World Models]], to the product-company framing (DeepMind Genie, AMI Labs, World Labs, NVIDIA Cosmos) — grounds the "simulator" and "planner" ends of the field's render/simulate/plan taxonomy in a decade of model-based RL research that predates the generative-video boom

## Notable work / recent moves
- Foundational lineage: "World Models" (Ha & Schmidhuber, NeurIPS 2018) established the train-in-imagination paradigm that PlaNet/Dreamer built on; Abbeel's lab has been a consistent collaborator/advisor across that line
- "World Model for Robot Learning: A Comprehensive Survey" (2026, co-authored) maps how video-generation-style world models and model-based-RL world models are converging for robotics
