## UCLA Shams Lab (Multisensory Processing Lab)

*Ladan Shams' lab at UCLA — the human/behavioral-science side of multisensory integration, and the computational-principles ground truth AI models get measured against.*

## What they do
- Studies how the brain integrates information from different sensory modalities into one coherent percept: which modalities interact, what neural mechanisms are involved, and what computational principles (e.g. reliability-weighted, Bayesian-style cue combination) govern the interaction
- Uses behavioral and neuroimaging studies in humans plus mathematical modeling, rather than building AI systems directly
- Also studies perceptual learning and adaptation — how multisensory integration itself changes with experience
- Directed by Ladan Shams (Psychology, Bioengineering, Neuroscience), funded by NSF/NIH/DARPA

## Where they fit
- Supplies the computational-neuroscience ground truth for [[Multisensory Integration]] — AI fusion architectures that claim to do "reliability-weighted" cross-modal combination are implicitly claiming to match findings that originate from labs like this one
- A useful check against "multisensory" branding: this lab's whole research program is about *how* biological systems weight and combine senses under uncertainty, which is exactly the part most AI multimodal systems currently skip
