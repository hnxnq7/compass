## Columbia Lightwave Research Laboratory (Keren Bergman)

*Keren Bergman's silicon-photonics group at Columbia — the leading academic lab on optical interconnects for datacenter and HPC-scale computing.*

## What they do
- Researches nanophotonic interconnect technologies (optical switching fabrics, networks-on-chip, chip-to-chip and chip-to-memory optical links) aimed specifically at energy-efficient, high-bandwidth datacenter and HPC systems
- Bergman directs the Center for Ubiquitous Connectivity (CUbiC), a 5-year multi-university center funded by DARPA and the Semiconductor Research Corporation, positioning the lab at the center of the DARPA/SRC-funded academic push on optical I/O
- Publishes extensively on the systems and networking side of optical interconnect (not just the device physics), i.e. how CPO and optical switching actually change datacenter topology and power budgets

## Where they fit
- The clearest academic anchor for [[Optical Interconnect & Co-Packaged Optics]] — sits upstream of commercial CPO efforts (NVIDIA, Broadcom, Ayar Labs), providing the systems-level research on where optical interconnect actually pays off in real datacenter architectures

## Notable work / recent moves
- Ongoing DARPA/SRC-funded CUbiC research (since 2023) on ubiquitous optical connectivity across compute scales
- Bergman is a frequent industry commentator (e.g. HPCwire/insideHPC podcasts) on when CPO moves from lab demo to production reliability
