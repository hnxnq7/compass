## Sakana AI

*Tokyo-based AI lab, founded by ex-Google researchers David Ha and Llion Jones, behind "The AI Scientist" (an end-to-end automated research pipeline) and "Evolutionary Model Merge" (automated, evolutionary-search model merging).*

## What they do
- Built The AI Scientist: hypothesis generation, literature review, experiment design, coding, execution, analysis, and paper-writing, fully automated with no human intervention
- v2 produced the first fully AI-generated paper to pass human peer review (ICLR 2025 ICBINB workshop)
- The methodology paper was itself published in Nature (March 2026) — a first for a fully automated AI research system
- Developed Evolutionary Model Merge, using evolutionary algorithms to automatically search for optimal ways to combine open-source model checkpoints instead of hand-tuning merge recipes

## Where they fit
- The most direct realization of the "AI Scientist" concept in [[AI for Scientific Discovery]] — closes the full loop from idea to published, peer-reviewed paper rather than stopping at hypothesis generation
- A research-driven counterpart to Arcee AI in [[Model Merging & Distillation]] — pushes merging toward automated evolutionary search rather than manual recipes, positioning merging existing open models as a compute-efficient alternative to training new foundation models from scratch

## Notable work / recent moves
- Nature publication validating the AI Scientist methodology (March 2026)
- Relicensed under a custom responsible-AI license (Dec 2025)
- Japan's most valuable startup — raised \$135M Series B in November 2025 at a \$2.65B valuation
