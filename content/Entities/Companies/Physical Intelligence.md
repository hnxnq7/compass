## Physical Intelligence

*Pure-play robot foundation model lab — betting on one general-purpose VLA model to control many different physical robots, rather than one model per robot/task.*

## What they do
- Builds a general-purpose Vision-Language-Action (VLA) foundation model intended to control any physical robot, not a single hardware platform
- Released three model generations in rapid succession: π0 (Oct 2024), π0.5, and π0.7 (public research release, April 2026) — each accepts camera images, joint angles, and natural-language commands and outputs motor commands
- Raised a \$600M Series B at a \$5.6B valuation, with over \$1B raised to date

## Where they fit
- One of the two most-cited labs (alongside Google DeepMind) for general-purpose VLA research in [[Vision-Language-Action Models & Embodied Robotics]] — the field's central "imitation vs. genuine world-model-informed action" tension plays out directly in how π0's successive generations are evaluated
- A useful contrast to robot-makers-that-also-train-models (Figure AI, Tesla): Physical Intelligence sells the model layer itself, betting that a model-agnostic-of-hardware approach wins over vertically integrated humanoid companies
