## Periodic Labs

*Pairs AI scientists with autonomous physical laboratories that run experiments end-to-end.*

## What they do
- Founded 2025 by Liam Fedus (co-creator of ChatGPT, former VP of Research at OpenAI) and Ekin Doğuş Çubuk (led the materials-discovery work behind Google DeepMind's GNoME)
- Stated north star is discovering a high-temperature superconductor; already has early paying customers in semiconductors, where faster materials-discovery cycles matter commercially

## Where they fit
- The clearest bet in [[AI for Scientific Discovery]] on "closed-loop" science — AI proposes, an automated lab executes, AI updates — in exactly the kind of fast-feedback physical-science domain the field's "what feels inevitable" section points to

## Notable work / recent moves
- Reported early revenue from semiconductor-industry customers (as of March 2026)
