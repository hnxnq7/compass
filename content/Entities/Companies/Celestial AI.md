## Celestial AI

*Silicon-photonics startup building "Photonic Fabric" — optical memory and interconnect fabric aimed at the same scale-up bottleneck Ayar Labs is chasing, from the memory-bandwidth side.*

## What they do
- Develops optical interconnect and memory-expansion technology (Photonic Fabric) meant to let accelerators pool HBM-class memory bandwidth across chips over light rather than copper
- Positions itself as solving the memory-bandwidth wall (see [[AI Accelerator Chips & Inference Silicon]]) via optical links rather than another HBM generation

## Where they fit
- A second, memory-bandwidth-first bet in [[Optical Interconnect & Co-Packaged Optics]] alongside Ayar Labs' I/O-first approach — useful to watch both since they're not solving quite the same problem

## Notable work / recent moves
- Raised \$520M+ total, including a \$255M round backed by TSMC's VentureTech Alliance and Samsung Catalyst Fund
- Targeting commercialization of its first silicon products in 2026
