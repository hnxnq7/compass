## CrewAI

*Open-source, role-based multi-agent framework built around a low-friction DSL — agents as a "crew" with defined roles rather than a hand-built graph.*

## What they do
- Framework for composing collaborative, role-based agent teams (a "crew"), with a much lower learning curve than graph-based alternatives — roughly 20 lines of DSL to get a working multi-agent setup
- Open-source core plus a commercial platform layer for deployment/monitoring
- \$18M Series A (Insight Partners, October 2024), backed by angels including Andrew Ng

## Where they fit
- The "low-friction, opinionated roles" end of the [[Multi-Agent Systems]] design space, a useful contrast to LangGraph's explicit-state-machine approach — evidence for the field's prediction that a small number of coordination patterns (role-based crews vs. explicit graphs) will dominate rather than continued bespoke design
- Adoption at real scale (reported by ~60% of Fortune 500, per company figures) makes it a signal for which coordination pattern is actually winning in production, not just in benchmarks

## Notable work / recent moves

- Reports ~1.4B agent executions/month as of 2026
