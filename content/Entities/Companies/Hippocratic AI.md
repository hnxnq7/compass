## Hippocratic AI

*Healthcare voice-agent company building clinically-scoped, non-diagnostic patient-facing AI.*

## What they do
- AI agents for pre-op, discharge planning, chronic care, clinical trial support, patient education, and appointment scheduling — deliberately scoped to non-diagnostic use cases
- Partners with large health systems, payors, and pharma clients rather than selling direct-to-consumer

## Where they fit
- Illustrates [[Enterprise AI & Vertical Copilots]]' point about regulated verticals layering compliance/safety requirements on top of the raw capability problem — the company's core pitch is safety scoping (non-diagnostic) as much as capability

## Notable work / recent moves
- \$126M Series C (Nov 2025) at a \$3.5B valuation
- Over 115M clinical patient interactions reported with no safety issues, across 50+ health systems/payors in six countries
