## Groq

*Maker of the LPU (Language Processing Unit), built specifically for low-latency inference — and now, oddly, half-absorbed by the company it was built to compete with.*

## What they do
- Designs deterministic, SRAM-heavy inference silicon (LPU) optimized for token-generation latency rather than training throughput
- Runs GroqCloud, a hosted inference service for open-weight models, across 13 datacenters worldwide

## Where they fit
- The other major data point (with Cerebras) in [[AI Accelerator Chips & Inference Silicon]] for "inference silicon as a genuinely different design target than training silicon" — but its trajectory now also illustrates consolidation pressure in the space

## Notable work / recent moves
- Entered a non-exclusive inference technology licensing agreement with NVIDIA in December 2025, reported at ~\$20B — Groq's founder and other leaders joined NVIDIA, while GroqCloud continues operating as a nominally independent company
- Raised \$650M in June 2026 to keep growing inference capacity (targeting 200MW by 2027) independent of the NVIDIA deal
