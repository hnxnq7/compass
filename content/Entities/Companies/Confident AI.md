## Confident AI

*Maker of DeepEval, a widely-adopted open-source LLM evaluation framework built heavily around LLM-as-judge metrics, plus a hosted platform on top.*

## What they do
- DeepEval: open-source Python/TypeScript framework with 50+ research-backed metrics (many LLM-as-judge-based, e.g. G-Eval), designed to run inside CI/CD pipelines rather than as a one-off notebook script
- Confident AI: hosted platform adding centralized test management, observability, and team collaboration on top of DeepEval

## Where they fit
- A concrete, high-adoption instance of LLM-as-judge moving from research technique to default CI/CD tooling — DeepEval reportedly runs millions of evaluations a day across its user base

## Notable work / recent moves
- Y Combinator W25; ~\$2M raised
- DeepEval: 12k+ GitHub stars, reported ~3M monthly downloads, cited enterprise users including Microsoft, BCG, AstraZeneca, and AXA
