## Renaissance Technologies

*Pioneering quant fund (Medallion Fund) using proprietary machine learning models; the archetype the field measures newer "AI hedge funds" against.*

## What they do
- Employs sophisticated mathematical/ML models and alternative data to find and exploit market inefficiencies across asset classes
- Famously secretive about methodology — public knowledge of its actual techniques is thin relative to its track record

## Where they fit
- The benchmark case for [[AI for Trading & Quant]]'s point that firms with genuine edge have zero incentive to publish it — Renaissance's results are the strongest evidence quant/ML-driven trading works, and also the least explained

## Notable work / recent moves
- Medallion Fund continues to be cited as the highest risk-adjusted-return fund in the industry's history

## Watch list
- No public blog/tracker — genuinely opaque; watch industry reporting (13F filings, court records) rather than owned channels
