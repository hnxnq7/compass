## Goodfire

*Startup built entirely around mechanistic interpretability — reverse-engineering models and selling programmatic access to their internals.*

## What they do
- Builds Ember, described as the first hosted mechanistic interpretability API — programmable access to a model's internal features for outside researchers/developers
- Ships "feature steering": tuning a model's internal features directly to shape its behavior, positioned as a more reliable alternative to prompting or fine-tuning for narrow behavior changes
- Team drawn substantially from DeepMind and OpenAI; works across modalities (text, vision, audio, genomic, robotic), not just LLMs
- Raised a \$150M round at a \$1.25B valuation (2026) to keep building interpretability-first tooling, following an earlier \$50M Series A

## Where they fit
- A pure-play commercial bet that [[Mechanistic Interpretability]] techniques (SAEs, feature discovery) are ready to be productized rather than staying research-only
- Ember's feature steering is a direct commercial instance of [[Activation Steering & Representation Engineering]] — the same underlying technique as academic steering-vector work, packaged as an API
