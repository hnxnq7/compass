## Oklo

*Sam Altman-backed small modular reactor company, and one of the clearest signs that "eventually SMRs" for datacenter power is now a contracted, dated bet rather than a hand-wave.*

## What they do
- Develops the Aurora Powerhouse, a 75MWe fast microreactor design, targeting direct power-purchase deals with datacenter operators instead of selling into the general grid
- Broke ground on its first-of-a-kind unit at Idaho National Laboratory in September 2025

## Where they fit
- The most concrete instance in [[Datacenter Power & Cooling Constraints]] of the "eventually SMRs" prediction — a signed, multi-gigawatt hyperscaler deal with a real construction timeline rather than a roadmap slide

## Notable work / recent moves
- Signed a 1.2GW power deal with Meta in January 2026 covering multiple Aurora Powerhouse units
- Targeting commercial operation of its first reactor in late 2027/early 2028 — worth watching whether that timeline holds, since SMR schedules have historically slipped
