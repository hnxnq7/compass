## Mostly AI

*Vienna-based synthetic data platform, one of the longest-running dedicated vendors in the space (founded 2017) — focused specifically on privacy-safe structured/tabular data rather than text or images.*

## What they do
- Generates synthetic tabular data that statistically mirrors real enterprise datasets (finance, healthcare, insurance) without exposing the underlying records, for use in testing, analytics, and model training under privacy constraints
- Founded 2017 by Klaudius Kalcher and Roland Boubela; ~42 employees as of 2026, \$31.1M raised (Series B) from Earlybird, 42 Capital, and weXelerate

## Where they fit
- A narrow but instructive case for [[Synthetic Data Generation & Model Collapse]]: structured/tabular synthetic data has a much cleaner "ground truth" (the real distribution being mimicked) than open-ended text generation, so it's a useful contrast case for where synthetic data works well vs. where the collapse risk (open-ended generative text/image models trained recursively on their own outputs) actually bites

## Notable work / recent moves
- Continues to operate as an independent Series B company as of 2026, distinct from acquisition-consolidated peers like Gretel (bought by NVIDIA in 2025)
