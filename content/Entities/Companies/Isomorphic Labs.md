## Isomorphic Labs

*DeepMind spinout applying AlphaFold-derived models to end-to-end AI drug design.*

## What they do
- Builds IsoDDE (released Feb 2026), a unified drug-design engine combining structure prediction, ligand binding, affinity prediction, and antibody interaction modeling in one pipeline — roughly doubles AlphaFold 3's accuracy on the hardest low-similarity ligand-binding cases
- Signed multi-target drug discovery deals with Eli Lilly (up to \$1.7B) and Novartis (up to \$1.2B)

## Where they fit
- The clearest large-scale bet that biomolecular foundation models (structure → function → design) can replace narrow, task-specific tools in [[AI for Biology, Genomics & Drug Discovery]] — CEO Demis Hassabis has said the goal is AI-designed drugs in clinical trials by end of 2026

## Notable work / recent moves
- Raised \$2.1B Series B (May 2026) to scale from research to applied drug design at scale
- IsoDDE unified pipeline (Feb 2026)
