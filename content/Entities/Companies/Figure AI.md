## Figure AI

*Full-stack humanoid robotics company — builds both the Figure 02 hardware and Helix, its own onboard VLA model.*

## What they do
- Designs and builds Figure 02 humanoid robot hardware in-house, rather than integrating a third-party robot platform
- Trains Helix, an onboard vision-language-action model that drives both arms and hands from vision and language input, giving Figure end-to-end control over the perception-to-actuation stack
- Represents the "robot maker trains its own model" camp of embodied AI, as distinct from pure-play model labs that sell a hardware-agnostic VLA model

## Where they fit
- A key data point in [[Vision-Language-Action Models & Embodied Robotics]] for whether vertical integration (one company owning both hardware and model) or a model-agnostic-of-hardware approach (Physical Intelligence, Google DeepMind) wins out for general-purpose robot competence
- Directly tests this field's "warehouse demo reel vs. general competence" skepticism — Helix's claims are evaluated against real humanoid deployment, not simulation

## Notable work / recent moves
- Helix VLA model controlling full upper-body dexterity (arms + hands) on Figure 02 hardware
