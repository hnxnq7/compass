## AMI Labs

*Yann LeCun's Paris-based startup, launched after leaving Meta — a direct bet against generative/autoregressive video models, built around his JEPA-style "world model" architecture instead.*

## What they do
- Founded by Yann LeCun after his departure from Meta following 12 years there; officially launched March 2026 with a \$1.03B seed round (~€890M, Europe's largest-ever seed) at a \$3.5B pre-money valuation, co-led by Cathay Innovation, Greycroft, Hiro Capital, HV Capital, and Bezos Expeditions, with NVIDIA, Samsung, and Toyota Ventures among backers
- Builds world models intended to understand basic physical-world principles from real-world data — explicitly positioned as an alternative to token-prediction/generative video models, aimed at powering drones, robotaxis, and other autonomous machines
- LeCun has argued publicly that current generative/LLM-style approaches are the wrong foundation for real-world physical understanding — AMI Labs is the commercial vehicle for that thesis

## Where they fit
- The highest-profile explicit counter-bet within [[Video Generation & World Models]] to the "video model as world model" approach (Sora/Veo/Kling-style) — AMI Labs' premise is that predictive, non-generative world models are the right substrate, echoing this field's own skepticism of "world model" branding applied to strong next-frame predictors
- Its target applications (drones, robotaxis, autonomous machines) also connect it to [[Vision-Language-Action Models & Embodied Robotics]], though the company itself is model-first, not robot-first
