## World Labs

*Fei-Fei Li's spatial-intelligence startup — building persistent, navigable 3D world models as a deliberate third front alongside language and vision models.*

## What they do
- Founded by Fei-Fei Li (Stanford); raised \$1.23B across two rounds since emerging from stealth in Sept 2024, including a \$1B round in Feb 2026 — backed by AMD, Autodesk, Emerson Collective, Fidelity, NVIDIA, and Sea
- Ships Marble, a frontier multimodal world model that generates high-fidelity, spatially coherent, persistent 3D environments from text prompts, images, video, or 360° panoramas
- Explicitly frames spatial intelligence as complementary to, not a subset of, language intelligence — Li's public position is "AGI will not be complete without spatial intelligence"

## Where they fit
- The clearest commercial and conceptual anchor for [[Spatial Intelligence]] — Marble is the reference example of a system explicitly targeting persistent, queryable 3D world representation rather than passive video generation
- Also relevant to [[Video Generation & World Models]] as the "renderer" end of Fei-Fei Li's render/simulate/plan taxonomy that field note uses to evaluate world-model claims

## Notable work / recent moves
- \$1B funding round, Feb 2026, at scale that signals heavy institutional bet on spatial intelligence as a distinct category
- Marble now in broader use by creators/designers/engineers for interactive 3D scene generation
