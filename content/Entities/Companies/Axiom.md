## Axiom

*Formal-verification startup building AxiomProver, generating machine-checked Lean proofs for enterprise code, finance, and chip design.*

## What they do
- Positions itself as an "autonomous mathematician" applied to enterprise verification problems, not just competition math
- AxiomProver produces full, machine-checkable Lean proofs; the company reported formal Lean solutions for all twelve 2025 Putnam problems with zero type-check errors
- Raised a \$200M round at a \$1.6B valuation (March 2026)

## Where they fit
- Applies the same formal-proof-search approach as the competition-math efforts in [[AI for Math Proofs]] to a commercial target (verifying enterprise code/systems), showing the overlap between this field and [[Software Verification]]/[[Hardware Verification]]
