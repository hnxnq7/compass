## Numenta

*Jeff Hawkins' neuroscience-inspired AI company — bets that cortical-column-style sparsity, not scale alone, is the path to more efficient and more brain-like machine intelligence.*

## What they do
- Founded 2005 by Jeff Hawkins, Donna Dubinsky, and Dileep George; builds machine intelligence based on the "Thousand Brains Theory" of how the neocortex computes
- Ships NuPIC 2.0, which applies neuroscience-derived sparsity, pruning, and quantization to transformer architectures to run LLMs efficiently on standard CPUs instead of GPUs — claimed 10-100x CPU throughput gains and up to 17-35x vs. NVIDIA A100s
- Has commercial partnerships with Intel, Oracle, F5, and Cequence validating the platform across data center, cloud, and edge deployments
- Spun the Thousand Brains Project out as an independent nonprofit in January 2025 to carry the more theoretical/open research forward

## Where they fit
- One of the longest-running "brain-inspired, not just brain-metaphor" companies in [[Neuro-AI & Brain-Inspired Models]] — their core bet (sparsity and cortical-column structure as a computational principle, not just an analogy) is a direct, shipping counterpoint to pure scaling-law approaches
- Commercially relevant beyond research: NuPIC is a concrete efficiency argument for why brain-inspired sparsity might matter to the field even if you don't buy the neuroscience

## Notable work / recent moves
- NuPIC 2.0 platform and partnerships with Intel, Oracle, F5, Cequence (ongoing as of 2026)
- Spun off the Thousand Brains Project as an independent 501(c)(3) nonprofit (Jan 2025) to house the open Thousand Brains Theory research separately from the commercial NuPIC product
