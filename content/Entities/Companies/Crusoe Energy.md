## Crusoe Energy

*Vertically integrated AI infrastructure company betting that behind-the-meter power generation, not grid interconnect, is the way to actually get gigawatt-scale datacenters built on schedule.*

## What they do
- Designs, builds, and operates AI-optimized datacenters paired with on-site power (stranded/waste gas, renewables, and increasingly dedicated gas turbines) so builds aren't gated by utility interconnection queues
- Runs a "Digital Renewable Optimization" platform to match datacenter siting to available behind-the-meter energy sources

## Where they fit
- The clearest commercial example in [[Datacenter Power & Cooling Constraints]] of the "on-site/behind-the-meter power" prediction actually being built at scale, not just discussed

## Notable work / recent moves
- Contracted 29 GE Vernova LM2500XPRESS gas turbines for roughly 1.21GW of on-site capacity across its datacenter portfolio, delivering mid-2026 through 2028
- Part of the broader behind-the-meter buildout that reached roughly 2GW of online capacity across four projects industry-wide as of mid-2026
