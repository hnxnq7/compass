## Recursion Pharmaceuticals

*Publicly traded "TechBio" company mapping disease biology at scale via high-throughput imaging and ML.*

## What they do
- Uses high-resolution microscopy plus machine learning to map disease biology across genetic, cellular, and animal models, integrated end-to-end as "Recursion OS"
- Advances its own pipeline (oncology, rare disease, neuroscience, immunology) alongside pharma partnerships

## Where they fit
- One of the longest-running proof points (or counterpoints) for [[AI for Biology, Genomics & Drug Discovery]]'s central tension — huge computational throughput vs. the still-unsolved question of whether any of it compresses time-to-market for an actual approved drug

## Notable work / recent moves
- Collaborations with Roche/Genentech, Bayer, Sanofi, and Merck KGaA collectively valued up to \$20B
- Trades on NASDAQ as RXRX; regularly reports pipeline and partnership updates via SEC filings
