## Human Native AI

*London data-licensing marketplace connecting rights holders with AI developers — infrastructure for paying creators for training-data use, rather than a technical attribution method.*

## What they do
- Runs a data licensing platform: rights-cleared content from publishers, authors, and other creators, licensed to AI developers through a single technical integration and standardized legal agreements
- Founded 2024 in London by James Smith and Jack Galilee (Smith previously at Google/Google DeepMind)
- Acquired by Cloudflare in January 2026 — Cloudflare's stated goal is making AI developers pay creators for content used in training, folding the marketplace into its broader "pay per crawl" infrastructure push

## Where they fit
- The clearest commercial answer to [[Data Attribution & Provenance]]'s licensing side — rather than solving technical attribution (which output came from which training example), it sidesteps the problem by making consent and payment happen at ingestion time
- Now part of Cloudflare's infrastructure play, which matters because Cloudflare sits in front of a large share of the web and can enforce licensing terms at the network level, not just the marketplace level

## Notable work / recent moves
- Raised ~\$3.56M seed funding (2024) from LocalGlobe and Mercuri before acquisition
- Acquired by Cloudflare, announced January 15, 2026
