## EvolutionaryScale

*Protein-language-model company founded by former members of Meta FAIR's ESM team.*

## What they do
- Builds ESM-3, a large protein language model that excels at generative/design tasks and implicit structure recovery — treats protein sequence, structure, and function as one modeling problem rather than three
- Founded 2023 by researchers who left Meta AI after building the original ESM (Evolutionary Scale Modeling) series there

## Where they fit
- Represents the "protein-native" foundation-model track in [[AI for Biology, Genomics & Drug Discovery]], distinct from Isomorphic Labs' structure-and-docking focus — closer to a general-purpose biomolecular LLM than a drug-design pipeline

## Notable work / recent moves
- ESM-3 (2024) remains a leading reference point alongside AlphaFold 3 and MIT's Boltz-2 in comparisons of biology foundation models
