## Cadence

*Major EDA vendor; pushing agentic AI into chip design and verification via its ChipStack AI Super Agent.*

## What they do
- Sells the core EDA toolchain for chip design and verification — Xcelium (logic simulation), Jasper (formal verification), Verisium (verification platform), Cerebrus (chip design)
- ChipStack AI Super Agent orchestrates multiple "virtual engineers" across that toolchain — writing testbenches, generating test plans, running regressions, debugging, and auto-fixing issues — built on NVIDIA Nemotron models
- Announced Level-5 (fully autonomous) agentic design capability at Computex 2026, with early access expected in H2 2026

## Where they fit
- One of the two dominant EDA incumbents (with [[Synopsys]]) now racing to make agentic AI verification a shipped product rather than a research demo — directly relevant to the "agentic, multi-step verification pipelines" trend called out in [[Hardware Verification]]
- Claims 40x faster RTL validation cycles and a five-week verification loop compressed to under a day; worth watching whether that holds up outside vendor benchmarks

## Notable work / recent moves
- ChipStack AI Super Agent launched 2026, integrating Verisium, Cerebrus, and JedAI under one agentic workflow
- Level-5 autonomous virtual engineer announced at Computex 2026, co-developed with NVIDIA
