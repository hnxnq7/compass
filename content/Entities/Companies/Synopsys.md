## Synopsys

*Major EDA vendor; Cadence's chief rival, building out AI copilots and agentic workflows across its verification stack under the Synopsys.ai / AgentEngineer banners.*

## What they do
- Sells the rival EDA toolchain to Cadence — VCS (simulation), VC Formal (formal verification), plus hardware-assisted verification (ZeBu/HAPS emulation)
- VSO.ai applies AI to verification-space optimization, aiming to reach coverage convergence faster; Formal Advisor generates formal testbenches from natural-language specs and improves convergence with iterative helper-property generation
- AgentEngineer is the broader push toward multi-agent, autonomously-executed engineering workflows; demoed an "L4 orchestrated, multi-agent, adaptive learning" chip design workflow at Synopsys Converge 2026

## Where they fit
- The other half of the EDA duopoly racing [[Cadence]] to productize agentic AI verification — reported customers (e.g. NVIDIA) claim 2-5x productivity gains from the AI copilots, though vendor-reported numbers deserve scrutiny same as Cadence's
- Directly relevant to [[Hardware Verification]]'s "agentic, multi-step verification pipelines" and "LLM-generated SVAs" threads — Formal Advisor is a concrete instance of the natural-language-spec-to-SVA pipeline the field note flags as genuinely hard

## Notable work / recent moves
- Formal Advisor and VSO.ai shipping inside VC Formal / Verdi / VS Code as of 2026
- AgentEngineer multi-agent workflow demoed at Synopsys Converge 2026
