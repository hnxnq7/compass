## Harmonic

*Builds Aristotle, a math-reasoning model that outputs formally verified Lean 4 proofs instead of free-text answers.*

## What they do
- Co-founded by Robinhood's Vlad Tenev; frames its work as "Mathematical Superintelligence" — answers that are formally checked, not just plausible
- Aristotle combines an LLM with Monte Carlo Tree Search, lemma guessing, and specialized geometry modules, verified step-by-step by Lean
- Ships Aristotle as both a consumer app and a public API

## Where they fit
- The most visible commercial bet in [[AI for Math Proofs]] that formal verification (not free-text chain-of-thought) is the right substrate for trustworthy AI mathematics

## Notable work / recent moves
- Aristotle reached gold-medal level on the 2025 IMO, formally verifying 5 of 6 problems
- Generated a formal proof for Erdős Problem #124
- Valued at ~\$1.45B (2025)
