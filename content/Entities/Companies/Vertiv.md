## Vertiv

*Datacenter power and thermal management vendor whose backlog is basically a real-time gauge of how fast liquid cooling is going from optional to mandatory.*

## What they do
- Builds direct-to-chip and immersion liquid cooling systems plus power infrastructure (UPS, switchgear) for hyperscale and AI datacenters
- Competes with Schneider Electric and Supermicro as one of the primary suppliers hyperscalers turn to as rack density outgrows air cooling

## Where they fit
- Concrete evidence for [[Datacenter Power & Cooling Constraints]]'s "liquid cooling becoming mandatory" prediction — its order backlog tracks the industry's cooling transition about as directly as any single company can

## Notable work / recent moves
- Ended 2025 with a ~\$15B backlog, up 109% year-over-year, attributed to AI-driven demand
- Opened a new manufacturing facility in Malaysia in 2026 to keep up with cooling-hardware demand

## Watch list
- Vertiv investor releases/earnings calls — backlog trend is a decent proxy for cooling-transition pace
