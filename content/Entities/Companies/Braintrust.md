## Braintrust

*Unifies production tracing/observability and structured evals into one workflow, with CI/CD quality gates before changes ship.*

## What they do
- Connects production traces, structured evals, and CI/CD gates so real failures become regression tests and releases can be blocked on quality thresholds
- Positions itself less as a monitoring dashboard and more as a "quality management system" for AI products — evaluation and observability treated as one connected concern, not separate tools

## Where they fit
- Directly embodies the note's "evals embedded in the deployment pipeline" prediction — eval-gated releases on every PR/prompt change, rather than a pre-launch-only checkpoint
