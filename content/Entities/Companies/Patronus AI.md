## Patronus AI

*SF-based enterprise eval company building proprietary judge models rather than just prompting a generic LLM to grade output.*

## What they do
- Builds proprietary evaluation/judge models: Lynx (hallucination detection) and GLIDER (general-purpose judge), plus Percival, an agent-failure debugger that detects 20+ agentic failure modes
- Launched the industry's first multimodal LLM-as-a-judge, for scoring image-to-text output (2025)

## Where they fit
- One of the few companies whose core product is the judge model itself rather than a harness around someone else's generic model — a direct bet that purpose-built judges beat ad hoc GPT-4-as-judge setups on the accuracy/cost/latency tradeoff

## Notable work / recent moves
- \$17M Series A (2024), ~\$20M total raised at the time, led by Notable Capital with Lightspeed Venture Partners and Datadog participating
- Multimodal LLM-as-a-judge launch (2025)
