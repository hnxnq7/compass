## General Intuition

*Trains spatial-temporal reasoning agents on hundreds of millions of hours of gameplay video — the bet that video games are the highest-leverage data source for teaching AI to act in space.*

## What they do
- Founded 2025, spun out of Medal (a gameplay-clip sharing platform); its initial training data is Medal's archive of uploaded gameplay footage paired with exact button-press/timing records
- Raised a \$320M Series A in June 2026 at a \$2.3B valuation (led by Khosla Ventures, with General Catalyst and Jeff Bezos participating), on top of a \$134M seed — became a unicorn in 2026
- Building foundation models and general agents for spatial reasoning, with funds earmarked for scaling compute via CoreWeave and opening broader API access by end of summer 2026

## Where they fit
- A distinct bet within [[Spatial Intelligence]] from World Labs' Marble: rather than generating persistent 3D scenes, General Intuition trains agents to *act* spatially, using gameplay as a cheap proxy for embodied, action-labeled spatial-temporal data
- Publicly framed by the founders as robotics' "ChatGPT moment" — i.e. spatial reasoning learned from games as a bridge to real-world robot control, which also connects it to [[Vision-Language-Action Models & Embodied Robotics]]
