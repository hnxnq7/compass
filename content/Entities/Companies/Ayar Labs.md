## Ayar Labs

*NVIDIA-backed optical I/O chiplet maker, and probably the best-funded pure-play bet that in-package optics for chip-to-chip links is close to real deployment rather than a lab demo.*

## What they do
- Builds optical I/O chiplets (TeraPHY) and light sources that replace copper links between chips with fiber, aimed at scale-up (chip-to-chip) bandwidth without the electrical power tax
- Licenses/integrates its optical engines into other vendors' ASIC designs rather than selling standalone chips

## Where they fit
- Direct evidence for [[Optical Interconnect & Co-Packaged Optics]]'s prediction that scale-up optical links follow scale-out once CPO yield matures — its funding and roadmap are explicitly timed to that transition

## Notable work / recent moves
- Closed a \$500M Series E in March 2026 at a \$3.75B valuation, backed by NVIDIA
- Expanding production/test capacity and opening a new office in Hsinchu, Taiwan, to scale manufacturing

## Watch list
