## Arcee AI

*Self-described "open intelligence lab" building small, efficient language models via model merging and distillation instead of pretraining from scratch — maintainers of MergeKit and DistillKit.*

## What they do
- Built and maintains MergeKit, the most widely-used open-source toolkit for merging LLM checkpoints, after acquiring it from original creator Charles Goddard
- Built DistillKit, an open-source toolkit for distilling large "teacher" models into smaller, cheaper student models
- Ships small language models (SLMs) that treat merging/distillation, not pretraining, as the primary path to capability

## Where they fit
- The clearest commercial proof point for [[Model Merging & Distillation]] — merging and distillation are their entire product thesis, not a side technique

## Notable work / recent moves
- MergeKit now supports vision-language architectures (LLaVA, QwenVL) alongside decoder-only models, and added tokensurgeon for cross-tokenizer merging/distillation
- Continued active open-source development of both MergeKit and DistillKit
