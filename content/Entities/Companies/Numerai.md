## Numerai

*Crowdsourced, blockchain-incentivized hedge fund — a global community submits ML models, Numerai ensembles the best into a meta-model that trades.*

## What they do
- Founded 2015; transforms market data into an anonymized ML prediction problem that thousands of data scientists compete on weekly
- Pays contributors in its native cryptocurrency (Numeraire/NMR) staked against the accuracy of their submitted models
- Secured up to \$500M from JPMorgan Asset Management (2026), roughly doubling AUM from ~\$450M

## Where they fit
- The clearest structural experiment in [[AI for Trading & Quant]] on *how* to source the ML layer — instead of hiring quants in-house, it crowdsources and ensembles models, a genuinely different organizational bet from Two Sigma/Renaissance

## Notable work / recent moves
- JPMorgan's \$500M commitment (2026)
- 25% net return in 2024, its strongest year to date at the time
