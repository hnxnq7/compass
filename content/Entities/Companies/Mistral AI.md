## Mistral AI

*French frontier lab, Europe's clearest open-weight alternative to closed US APIs — Mixtral 8x7B was one of the models that made MoE the open-weight default rather than a dense-model niche.*

## What they do
- Releases both open-weight (Mistral 7B, Mixtral) and commercial (Mistral Large, Codestral via La Plateforme) models
- Mixtral 8x7B (Dec 2023) was among the first widely-used open-source sparse MoE models, showing dense-model-beating quality at a fraction of the active-parameter cost
- Continuing to iterate on MoE design — CEO Arthur Mensch has described newer releases as "fat but sparse," i.e. large total capacity, small active compute

## Where they fit
- Helped establish [[Mixture-of-Experts & Sparse Scaling]]'s "MoE as the default architecture" pattern in the open-weight ecosystem, ahead of most competitors
- Backed by a large compute buildout (reportedly ~€4B datacenter investment) that keeps them relevant to [[Model Architecture Research]] as one of the few labs outside the US/China able to validate architecture choices at frontier scale
- Runs the open/closed bifurcation as an active commercial and sovereignty strategy for [[Open vs Closed Weight Governance]] — CEO Arthur Mensch frames open weights as "strategic autonomy," and Mistral signed both the EU's GPAI Code of Practice and the July 2026 US industry letter opposing broad open-weight restrictions

## Notable work / recent moves
- Mixtral 8x7B / 8x22B — open-weight sparse MoE releases that popularized the architecture outside frontier labs
- New MoE model family in early access as of July 2026, alongside a €4B+ datacenter buildout
