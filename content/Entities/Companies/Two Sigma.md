## Two Sigma

*Long-running quant hedge fund (founded 2001) now running an explicit "AI-first" internal mandate.*

## What they do
- Systematic/quantitative investing using large-scale data science; has used NLP in research for over a decade and has moved to generative AI in its investment research process
- 2026 "AI-first" mandate frames AI as a universal layer across research, data engineering, compliance, and operations — not just a tool for a specialist team — chasing what the firm calls "operational alpha"

## Where they fit
- A concrete illustration of [[AI for Trading & Quant]]'s central thesis: the edge stays classical/quantitative, with the LLM layer applied to research productivity and operations rather than the trading decision itself

## Notable work / recent moves
- Spectrum and Absolute Return Enhanced funds posted double-digit 2024 returns
- Senior AI hire from Goldman Sachs (2026) to build out the AI-first program
