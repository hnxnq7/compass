## Gray Swan AI

*Automated adversarial red-teaming company founded out of Carnegie Mellon; runs the largest public AI red-teaming network.*

## What they do
- Founded by AI safety/security researchers from Carnegie Mellon, including Zico Kolter and Matt Fredrikson
- Runs Gray Swan Arena, described as the world's largest adversarial AI red-teaming network, powered by 15,000+ red teamers
- Sells "AI Red-Teaming as a Service" — automated adversarial testing, continuous red teaming, and runtime AI security for deployed models/agents
- Partnered with the UK AI Security Institute and multiple frontier labs on a large-scale public AI agent red-teaming competition (results covered by NIST/CAISI)

## Where they fit
- A commercial, at-scale instance of [[Behavioral Red-Teaming as Interpretability Proxy]] — crowdsourced adversarial probing used as the primary (behavioral, not mechanistic) evidence of an agent's attack surface

## Notable work / recent moves
- Large-scale public AI agent red-teaming competition with UK AISI (results covered by NIST/CAISI blog)
- Gray Swan Arena ongoing adversarial testing platform
