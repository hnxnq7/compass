## Cerebras

*Builds the wafer-scale engine — a single dinner-plate-sized chip instead of thousands of small ones — and has pivoted hard into selling inference speed.*

## What they do
- Designs the Wafer Scale Engine (WSE), keeping an entire model's working set on one enormous die to avoid the chip-to-chip communication overhead that constrains GPU clusters
- Increasingly sells this as an inference product (fast token generation for open-weight models) rather than just a training accelerator, competing directly with Groq on latency

## Where they fit
- The clearest counter-example to "just scale GPU clusters" in [[AI Accelerator Chips & Inference Silicon]] — a genuinely different architectural bet (one giant die vs. many networked chips) that's found a real niche rather than displacing NVIDIA

## Notable work / recent moves
- IPO'd in 2026, stock jumped ~68% on debut; raised roughly \$1B at a ~\$23B valuation
- Positioning inference speed (not raw FLOPs) as the headline metric against NVIDIA and Groq
