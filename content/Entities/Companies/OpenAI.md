## OpenAI

*Frontier AI lab; authored the foundational research establishing process reward models as superior to outcome-only supervision for reasoning tasks.*

## What they do
- Published "Let's Verify Step by Step" and open-sourced PRM800K, a large human-annotated step-level label dataset for mathematical reasoning — the field's foundational PRM dataset
- Showed PRMs trained on step-level labels beat outcome-only reward models (ORMs) on Best-of-K reasoning performance

## Where they fit
- The origin point for [[Process & Outcome Reward Models]] as a named research direction — PRM800K remains a standard reference dataset that later open efforts (Math-Shepherd, RLHFlow, Qwen PRM) build on or compare against

## Notable work / recent moves
- PRM800K dataset and "Let's Verify Step by Step" paper remain among the most-cited foundational references in the PRM literature
