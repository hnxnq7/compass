## Arize AI

*AI observability company monitoring model and agent quality, drift, and failures on live production traffic.*

## What they do
- Platform (including the open-source Phoenix project) for tracing, monitoring, and evaluating LLM/agent behavior on real production traffic — quality, drift, hallucinations, latency, and cost in one operational layer

## Where they fit
- One of the most established platforms built specifically to close the lab-to-deployment gap this note describes, by evaluating against real production traffic instead of only static curated sets

## Notable work / recent moves
- Founded 2020, based in Mill Valley, CA; widely cited alongside LangSmith and Braintrust as one of the three leading LLM observability/eval platforms heading into 2026
