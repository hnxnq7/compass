## RLHFlow

*Open-source reward-modeling and RLHF pipeline project out of UIUC (Tong Zhang, Wei Xiong) and Salesforce AI Research.*

## What they do
- Publishes open recipes, code, and trained models for process- and outcome-supervised reward models (PRM/ORM) and online iterative RLHF
- Aims to make reward-model training reproducible for teams without proprietary human preference data, using constructed proxy preference/process signal instead

## Where they fit
- One of the main open-source counterweights to closed PRM work (e.g. OpenAI's PRM800K) in [[Process & Outcome Reward Models]] — widely reused as a base recipe in other papers' reward-model training

## Notable work / recent moves
- RLHF-Reward-Modeling repo and "RLHF Workflow: From Reward Modeling to Online RLHF" paper cited as a reproducible baseline recipe across 200+ downstream research projects
