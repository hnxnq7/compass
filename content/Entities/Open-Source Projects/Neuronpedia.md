## Neuronpedia

*Open-source interpretability platform for exploring, visualizing, and steering the internals of AI models — created by Johnny Lin.*

## What they do
- Hosts explanations, activations, and metadata for SAE/transcoder features across multiple open models, browsable by anyone (5+ TB of activations/explanations)
- Provides a public interpretability-and-steering API — lets users trace a model's internal reasoning on custom prompts, and modify behavior by steering activations via latents or custom vectors
- Fully open-sourced its codebase (2025), after starting as a closed feature-explorer in early 2024
- Partnered with Google DeepMind to host the public interactive demo for the Gemma Scope release

## Where they fit
- The de facto shared front-end for [[Mechanistic Interpretability]] work — where SAE releases (like Gemma Scope) get an interactive public demo instead of staying a raw weights dump
- Its steering API is a working, openly accessible instance of [[Activation Steering & Representation Engineering]], available to anyone without needing their own SAE infrastructure
