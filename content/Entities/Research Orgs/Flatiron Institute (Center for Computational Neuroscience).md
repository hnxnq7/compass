## Flatiron Institute — Center for Computational Neuroscience (CCN)

*Simons Foundation-funded research center running PI-led groups at the boundary of neuroscience and AI — not a university lab, but structured and staffed like one.*

## What they do
- Nonprofit research institute (part of the Simons Foundation) with core groups spanning computational vision (Eero Simoncelli), neural circuits and algorithms (Dmitri "Mitya" Chklovskii), NeuroAI and geometric data analysis (SueYeon Chung), and statistical analysis of neural data (Alex Williams)
- The NeuroAI and Geometric Data Analysis group specifically studies computation in brains and artificial neural networks by analyzing the geometry of neural/feature representations and developing network models and learning rules guided by neuroscience
- Maintains open-source software for computational/systems neuroscience (NeuroRSE), and runs postdoctoral fellowships explicitly scoped to NeuroAI

## Where they fit
- A hub for the specific sub-question in [[Neuro-AI & Brain-Inspired Models]] of whether representational geometry — how neural populations (biological or artificial) organize information — is a shared computational currency between brains and deep nets, rather than a coincidence of high-capacity models
- Philanthropically funded and PI-led like a university lab, but institutionally independent, which lets it hire specifically around the neuroscience/ML boundary rather than within a single department

## Notable work / recent moves
