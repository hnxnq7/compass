## Project Numina

*Nonprofit building open agentic systems that solve competition and research math with formal (Lean) verification.*

## What they do
- Builds Numina-Lean-Agent, an open, general agentic reasoning system for formal math; solved all 12 problems of the 2025 Putnam using Claude Opus 4.5 as the base model
- Received a €3M research grant from XTX Markets (a quant trading firm) to fund the work

## Where they fit
- The clearest open/nonprofit counterweight to closed commercial efforts (Harmonic, Axiom, DeepMind's AlphaProof) in [[AI for Math Proofs]] — publishes its agent framework rather than keeping it proprietary

## Notable work / recent moves
- Numina-Lean-Agent solving all 12 Putnam 2025 problems (2026)
