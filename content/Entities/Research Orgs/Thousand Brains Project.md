## Thousand Brains Project

*Independent nonprofit spun out of Numenta to carry the open "Thousand Brains Theory" research and its Monty sensorimotor-learning framework forward outside a commercial roadmap.*

## What they do
- Advances the Thousand Brains Theory — the idea that the neocortex runs thousands of largely-independent, cortical-column-sized modeling units in parallel, each building its own model of an object/concept via sensorimotor interaction, with perception emerging from their combination
- Provides an open-source sensorimotor learning framework (Monty) implementing these ideas, positioned as "a fundamentally different approach to AI" from transformer-style scaling
- Launched as an independent 501(c)(3) nonprofit in January 2025, separating the open theoretical research from Numenta's commercial NuPIC product line

## Where they fit
- A rare case in [[Neuro-AI & Brain-Inspired Models]] of a research agenda that's both explicitly biologically grounded (named after an actual neuroscience theory of cortical computation) and shipping open, usable code rather than staying purely theoretical
- Directly tests this field's hardest question — whether a model built around neurobiological principles (sensorimotor, modular, reference-frame-based) can do real work, not just predict neural activity
