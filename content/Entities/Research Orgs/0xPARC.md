## 0xPARC

*Research and education organization for programmable cryptography — zero-knowledge proofs, MPC, FHE, obfuscation — closely tied to the Ethereum ecosystem.*

## What they do
- Runs research, learning residencies, hackathons, and conferences on zero-knowledge cryptography and "autonomous worlds" (decentralized application design)
- Research agenda spans zero-knowledge proofs, multiparty computation, fully homomorphic encryption, and obfuscation
- Awards research grants and coordinates cross-team technical projects across companies and research groups in the space; the Ethereum Foundation is their largest supporter

## Where they fit
- The clearest research-org anchor for [[Zero-Knowledge & Programmable Cryptography]] — most of the field's applied momentum currently comes from the crypto/blockchain ecosystem specifically, and 0xPARC is one of the main hubs coordinating it
- Not an AI lab, but relevant to this atlas because programmable cryptography (proving a computation was done correctly, without revealing inputs) is structurally a formal-verification problem — worth tracking alongside [[Proof Assistants & Formal Mathematics]] and [[Software Verification]] even though the community and funding are distinct

## Notable work / recent moves
- Published *Programmable Cryptography: Four Easy Pieces*, an intro book covering 2PC, SNARKs, FHE, and ORAM (edited by Brian Lawrence, Evan Chen, Yan X. Zhang; github.com/0xPARC/0xparc-intro-book)
- Runs research workshops (with the Ethereum Foundation and Stanford Center for Blockchain Research) that produce concrete open-problem results — the 2025 workshop yielded an algebraic digit-extraction approach and two substring-matching solutions, following a 2024 workshop that found a counterexample to a proximity-gap conjecture
