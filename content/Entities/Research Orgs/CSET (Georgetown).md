## CSET (Center for Security and Emerging Technology)

*Georgetown University think tank producing the empirical research US policymakers actually cite on AI, compute, and national security.*

## What they do
- Founded 2019 (Open Philanthropy-backed) to bring rigorous, nonpartisan analysis of AI and emerging technology to US national security policy
- Publishes on compute governance, chip supply chains, and AI-China competition; briefs Congress, DoD, and other federal agencies directly
- Maintains the AGORA dataset mapping AI governance documents across jurisdictions (1,000+ documents as of its April 2026 landscape update)

## Where they fit
- One of the most-cited sources feeding US federal AI policy, particularly on the compute thresholds and export-control questions that run through [[AI Policy & Regulation]]
- Its governance-landscape mapping work is a direct empirical input to the "1,000+ initiatives, no convergence" problem the field note describes

## Notable work / recent moves
- "Mapping the AI Governance Landscape" — April 2026 update expanding the AGORA dataset
- Ongoing compute/chip supply-chain policy research feeding export-control debates
