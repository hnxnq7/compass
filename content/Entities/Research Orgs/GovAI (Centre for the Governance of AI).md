## GovAI (Centre for the Governance of AI)

*Oxford-affiliated research center that helped establish AI governance as its own research discipline.*

## What they do
- Independent research nonprofit (originally incubated at Oxford's Future of Humanity Institute) focused on how societies navigate the transition to advanced AI
- Core research areas: compute governance, international coordination mechanisms, and structuring durable AI treaties/frameworks
- Runs fellowship programs (Research Track, Policy Track) that have trained a large share of the people now working across lab policy teams, national AI safety institutes, and other think tanks

## Where they fit
- Foundational research on compute thresholds and coordination mechanisms underpins the regulatory-trigger approaches [[AI Policy & Regulation]] describes as "already the observable pattern"
- Functions as much as a talent pipeline as a research shop — alumni network extends directly into CSET and government AI policy roles

## Notable work / recent moves
- 2026 Summer Research Fellowship cohort
- Ongoing publication series on compute governance and international coordination (governance.ai/research)
