## FutureHouse

*Philanthropically-funded nonprofit building semi-autonomous "AI Scientist" systems to accelerate biomedical research.*

## What they do
- Founded 2023 by Sam Rodriques and Andrew White, based in San Francisco
- Built Kosmos, an AI Scientist that conducts complex analyses autonomously — claims roughly six months of research compressed into a single day at ~80% accuracy
- Launched a public platform (platform.futurehouse.org) putting these agents in front of scientists via web UI and API
- Runs an AI-for-Science Independent Postdoctoral Fellowship pairing early-career researchers with AI tools and academic co-advisors

## Where they fit
- Defines its mission explicitly around the "AI Scientist" framing central to [[AI for Scientific Discovery]] — input a research direction, output experimental results/analysis/a paper — with biomedical research as the primary domain, overlapping [[AI for Biology, Genomics & Drug Discovery]]

## Notable work / recent moves
- Kosmos launch (2025) and Platform launch making the agents publicly available (2026)
- AI-for-Science Fellowship cohort starting September 2026
