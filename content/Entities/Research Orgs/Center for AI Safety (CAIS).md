## Center for AI Safety (CAIS)

*San Francisco nonprofit (founded 2022 by Dan Hendrycks and Oliver Zhang) that authored the founding "representation engineering" paper and builds widely-used AI safety benchmarks.*

## What they do
- Technical AI safety and AI ethics research, field-building, and advocacy, directed by Dan Hendrycks
- Authored "Representation Engineering: A Top-Down Approach to AI Transparency" — the first paper to control model internals to make models lie or be honest, demonstrating steering toward truthfulness, power-aversion, and ethical behavior
- Builds widely-cited safety benchmarks, including WMDP (Weapons of Mass Destruction Proxy, with Scale AI), used to measure and enable unlearning of hazardous knowledge

## Where they fit
- The origin point of "representation engineering" as a named, top-down alternative to bottom-up circuit analysis within [[Activation Steering & Representation Engineering]] — their framing (control high-level representations directly rather than individual features) shapes how the field talks about the technique
