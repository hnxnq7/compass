## Cross-cutting notes
- 2026 is the year governance stopped being hypothetical: the EU AI Act becomes fully enforceable August 2, 2026, and NIST launched a dedicated autonomous-agent standards initiative in February 2026 — the regulatory surface is now agents specifically, not just models.
- Insurance is becoming an informal enforcement mechanism ("AI Security Riders" requiring documented red-teaming) faster than law in some markets.
